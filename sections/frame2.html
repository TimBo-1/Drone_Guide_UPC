06 · Integration

Frame assembly — part 2

Mount the autopilot, connect the motors, and add the GPS, telemetry radio and buzzer. After this, the airframe is complete (propellers go on only at flight time).

Helpful walkthrough for this activityYouTube · reference

  1. Fix the autopilot to the top plate

    Centre it precisely

    It is crucial that the autopilot is perfectly centred: the geometric centre of the orange cube must coincide with the geometric centre of the plate. An off-centre IMU degrades flight stability. (See the video at ~4:30.)

  2. Connect the receiver & power the autopilot

    The SBUS OUT → RCIN cable both carries commands and powers the receiver. Mind the polarity of the three pins. Power the autopilot itself through the POWER2 cable from the distribution board. Figure 6.4

  3. Connect the motors to the autopilot

    Motors are numbered as in Figure 6.5 (left); connect each to its matching MAIN OUT output (right). The front motors' cable extensions can help, though a direct connection keeps the wiring tidier. Figure 6.5

  4. Install the GPS receiver

    Mount it at the front of the top plate. Route the black cable inside the support. Connect the red/black connector to CAN1; also connecting the white/green connector to CAN2 is recommended. Figure 6.6

  5. Install the telemetry radio

    One transceiver plugs into the laptop's USB; the other connects to the autopilot's Telem1 input. This link lets Mission Planner talk to the drone in flight, which the USB cable cannot. Figure 6.7

  6. Install the buzzer

    The buzzer connects to the autopilot's USB input. Figure 6.8

  7. Add Velcro & straps for the battery

    Fit the hook-and-loop strips and straps that will hold the battery, with extra Velcro as backup. Figure 6.9

  8. Secure everything

    Fasten the receiver and telemetry radio with Velcro and zip ties so nothing shifts in flight. Figure 6.10
