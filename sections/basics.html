The very basics of a drone and its components

A simple but complete mental model of what a drone is and what each part does — so every later activity makes sense.

Before touching a single screw, it helps to see the whole picture: how the transmitter, receiver, electronic speed controllers (ESCs), brushless motors, autopilot and GPS fit together. The introductory video gives that global view; the complementary videos go deeper into any concept you want to revisit later. You do not need to watch all of them up front — keep them as a reference.
