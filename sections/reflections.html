Team reflections

What we are taking away from the Transversal Project — the skills, the surprises and the teamwork.

What we learned

UAV architecture: Understanding the main components and their interactions within a multirotor UAV.

Practical assembly: Gaining hands-on experience assembling and connecting the HEXSOON EDU450.

Configuration & troubleshooting: Learning the importance of correct connections, configuration, testing, and systematic troubleshooting.

Teamwork & integration: Developing teamwork skills and connecting theoretical knowledge with a real UAV system.

What surprised us

How much small details matter. We were surprised that something as simple as one reversed wire, one loose connection, or one incorrect parameter could prevent the entire UAV from working properly. It showed us how important precision and systematic checking are when working with UAV systems.

The complexity of UAV configuration. We were surprised by the large number of parameters that can be adjusted in Mission Planner. This gave us a more intuitive understanding of how precise and complex a UAV system is, even for what may seem like a simple flight mission.

How we worked

Worked step by step: We followed the assembly instructions and completed the UAV systematically, from the frame and motors to the electronics and flight controller.

Checked each other’s work: We cross-checked connections, component installation, and parameters to reduce mistakes.

Tested progressively: Instead of testing everything at once, we checked the system step by step and solved problems as they appeared.

Communicated and learned together: When someone encountered a problem, we discussed it as a team and shared what we learned with each other.

Improvements for next time

Investigate open-ended problems together: When facing problems that are not clearly addressed in the guide, we should discuss them as a team, investigate different possibilities, and find solutions together.

Follow the guide more carefully: When a problem or procedure is already explained in the guide, we should read it carefully and follow the instructions step by step instead of assuming we already know the answer and skipping steps.

Be less overconfident: This experience showed us that even familiar-looking procedures can contain important details, so we should verify the instructions before relying on our assumptions.
